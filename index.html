Platform Features

Everything You Need to Manage Daily Contributions

Comprehensive tools designed specifically for personal financial discipline, rotating credit groups, and cooperative thrift societies.

Daily Contribution Tracking

Log deposits with a single click. Configure custom schedules—daily, bi-weekly, or weekly—and instantly monitor your running personal balance.

Automated streak & tally

Payment Verification

Dual-verification protocol allows members to attach transfer references or bank slips. Coordinators approve deposits with timestamped verification badges.

Zero dispute reconciliation

Contribution Reminders

Automated notifications sent prior to daily cutoff times so participants never forget a contribution. Reduces late payments and maintains circle health.

Smart SMS and email nudges

Group Management

Easily configure rotating payouts (ROSCAs, Esusu, Chit Funds), pooled funds, or targeted savings. Assign roles, invite trusted peers, and customize payout dates.

Flexible rotation rules

Savings Analytics

Gain clarity on collective savings velocity, member discipline scores, cumulative monthly yields, and projected completion targets through visual charts.

Real-time financial telemetry

Downloadable Reports

Export structured CSV and PDF contribution statements for tax planning, cooperative general meetings, or personal accounting with a single click.

Audit-ready statement exports
Simple Onboarding

How ContriTrack Works in Four Simple Steps

Get your individual discipline or collaborative savings circle up and running in less than five minutes.

01

Create Your Account

Sign up with your email or phone number in under 60 seconds. Set your profile and define your initial savings goals.

02

Join or Create a Group

Launch a private savings circle or join an existing cooperative using a secure group invitation code.

03

Submit Your Contributions

Deposit your daily dues, enter your payment reference, and receive immediate verification receipts from the coordinator.

04

Track Your Savings

Monitor your personal growth, track circle milestones in real-time, and collect your guaranteed lump-sum payout on schedule.
